Profile agent-tool: RE4exa ships three agent-first CLIs and MCP servers (rex, dissect, rqs) with skills, and the standard itself names rex and dissect as agent-tool examples (standard/RQS-2030.md:94); the suggested workflow-engine came from the word 'durable' (tools/rqs/rqs/suggest.py:61-63). Flags: uses_llm (MCP servers and skills), executes_code (rex ext and run start external programs; dissect runs node, go and git), parses_untrusted (binaries, archives, repositories, bundles), uses_crypto (SHA-256 content addresses verified on import, tools/rex/rex/store.py:209-215); network_service and heavy_compute are off because every probe hit for them is rqs's own pattern table (tools/rqs/rqs/scan.py:151-159), distributed is off (no package, release or tag). Reviewed commit 0bdc987 via git show and git archive; HEAD moved to 82e1713 during the review (review files plus compensated summation in score.py), and validation used that rqs.

- Question: Does RE4exa meet its own RQS-2030 bar? No: grade D (QI 38.1, coverage 100%), capped by two confirmed red lines (RL-1 no license, RL-4 injection-prone bundle import); its strengths are rex's evidence model, durable steps and small catalogs, its weaknesses the absence of CI, policies, test strength and agent-safety controls.
- Question: Do the README claims match the results they cite? For REA's numbers mostly yes (exact matches on catalog bytes, timings, export size, graph counts); for rex's own several do not (4.0 s vs 4.48 s; 'measured fixes' that were not measured; 0.42 s, 52-70 ms and 4,607 bytes with no committed result; 'standard-library Python' vs PyYAML).
- Question: How accurate is rqs on its own repository (G14)? Poor: 19 of 85 probes report found=true on rqs's pattern tables or the standard's prose alone, 9 of 39 levelled suggestions rest only on such lines, 2 of 8 suggested flags and the suggested profile are wrong, 17 of the 39 automatic levels had to change, and the real evidence (numeric claims, harness and results, describe commands, the dynamic SQL in rex) is missed.
- Not examined: The research harnesses were not re-run (REA package, Postgres crash test, caveman and leanrea lenses, product-eval.py); their committed outputs were checked against the READMEs instead.; Exploitability of the rex import SQL construct and of the parser resource limits was not demonstrated (defensive review rules).; Whether the git subcommands dissect runs inside scanned repositories (tools/dissect/dissect/extract.py:66-70, without the hardening rqs applies at scan.py:372) honour repository configuration that starts programs was not tested.; The exact size of rex's MCP catalog (4,607 bytes claimed) was not recomputed; only the test bound of 12,000 bytes was run.; The dissection database was checked for counts, kinds and secrets only; the 603 claim nodes and the curated layer were not audited one by one.; macOS, Windows and Python 3.11-3.12 behaviour (only Linux with Python 3.13.16 was used).; GitHub-side settings (branch protection, pull-request review) are not visible from the checkout.; The standard's regulatory dates and references were not checked against primary sources (no web access).
